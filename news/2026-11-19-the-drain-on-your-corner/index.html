West Seattle · One useful thing

the drain on your corner

November 19, 2026 · West Seattle Dispatch, issue 1 · All West Seattle news

Leaves are the first thing that clogs a storm drain, and a clogged drain is how a rainy night turns into water across the street or in a basement. Seattle Public Utilities' Adopt-a-Drain asks for fifteen minutes a month: rake the leaves and trash off the grate of one drain near you, leaves into the yard waste cart. As of October 2024, 2,044 volunteers had adopted 3,372 drains. Seattle Public Utilities
