How it works

  1. Request a Thursday. Pick a placement and a date, or let us suggest the next open one.
  2. We write the copy with you. You send your logo, your link and the one thing readers should know; the draft comes back the same day for your OK.
  3. Pay online. Your invoice arrives by email within minutes of the request, with a link to pay by card or bank transfer. It is paid before the issue runs.
  4. See what it did. Within 48 hours of each issue: how many readers opened it and how many clicked your link.

Our standards

  • Every paid placement is labeled "Sponsored".
  • Local businesses only. No real estate brokerages or agents, no political ads.
  • Ads never buy editorial coverage.
  • Readers' email addresses are never sold and never given to advertisers.
