Whether it is getting paid street parking, and when

November 26, 2026 · West Seattle Dispatch, issue 2 · Every answered question

A reader in the Junction asked whether it is getting paid street parking, and when. Not before 2027. SDOT's draft plan would make the busiest blocks 2-hour paid parking, 8 AM to 8 PM, Monday through Saturday: California Avenue SW from SW Dakota to SW Edmunds, plus stretches of 42nd and 44th Avenues SW, SW Genesee, SW Oregon, the south side of SW Alaska, Fauntleroy Way SW and SW Edmunds. Proposed starting rates are $1 an hour before 11 AM and $4 after. California south of Edmunds and 40th and 41st Avenues SW would stay free with 2-hour limits, extended to 8 PM. SDOT drew more than 480 comments this fall, plans to share the final version this winter and to install it in 2027; the project inbox is AlaskaJunctionParking@seattle.gov.
